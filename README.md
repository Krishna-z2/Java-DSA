## Day 6 — Reverse a Number

The **Reverse a Number** problem was a pretty amazing question that helps me to explains the Mathematical Understanding.
Initially, I Struggled with Updating variables inside the loop and building the reversed number correctly. After trying  2 or 3 Times and Taking Hints from AI , I understood:

- How `% 10` extracts the last digit of a number.
- How `/ 10` removes the last digit.
- How to build the reversed number using `reversed = reversed * 10 + remainder`.
- Why updating the loop variable is important to avoid infinite loops.
- Why leading zeros disappear when reversing an integer (e.g., `1000` becomes `1`).

**Key Takeaway:** This problem helped me improve my logical thinking, debugging skills, and understanding of `while` loops. Solving it step by step was more valuable than Simply Memorizing the Solution.
